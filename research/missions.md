## Counting rules

- `M01`–`M22` are mission-level records.
- `A01`–`A03` are explicitly marked assets or portfolio placeholders and should not be counted as independent lunar landings without a later source update.
- Artemis III is retained because it is an upcoming lunar-program mission, but its current objective is a low-Earth-orbit systems demonstration rather than a lunar surface landing.
- The Draper/CP-12 record is intentionally not duplicated as a separate ispace Mission 5 row; the provider's revised schedule says the same CP-12 work is proposed as Mission 5 in 2030, pending NASA approval.
